# SOURCE AND RIGHTS REPORT

Generated 2026-11-16 for the Kundu Electronics product image library.

## 1. Method

Every one of the 211 catalogue products was researched separately:

1. The product was identified from the catalogue name using its model/part number, package,
   pin count, capacity, voltage and variant description (`evidence/research-log.csv`, columns
   `canonical_name`, `manufacturer`, `model_or_part`, `identification_basis`).
2. Sources checked per product: the TechShopBD product index (1,594 catalogued product photos),
   Wikimedia Commons (a long descriptive query and a short model-focused query), Openverse's CC
   image index for distinctive items, plus manufacturer, distributor and specialist retailer pages
   where the part is identifiable. Where those indexes had no match, a further web image-search
   pass was run with exact model numbers, alternate names, translated terms and technical
   descriptions, and every candidate was inspected at full resolution before use. Result counts
   and top matching titles are recorded in the research log.
3. Candidates were inspected against the identification notes (connector type, pin count, board
   markings, printed rating). Visual similarity alone was never accepted: a different model,
   package, capacity or C-rating was rejected even when the photo looked similar.
4. Every retained image was downloaded at full resolution, background-removed, composited on pure
   white, and then reviewed again visually as a processed file.

## 3. Sourcing and logo policy

The brief for this pass was coverage with accuracy: manufacturer sites, distributors, electronics
retailers and other public sources may all be used, and an unclear commercial-reuse licence is not
by itself a reason to reject a correct photo. Those files are marked `Permission unknown` rather
than dropped, so the owner can decide on permission and credit later.

What is never acceptable, regardless of licence, is a third-party mark: any visible company logo,
store logo, watermark, banner or promotional graphic disqualifies the file. Product model numbers
and technical markings printed on the component itself (for example `LM2596S`, `74HC595N`, `104`)
are kept, because they are part of the product and help the customer identify it.

## 4. Attribution requirements

- CC BY 2.0 / 3.0 / 4.0: credit the photographer and the licence.
- CC BY-SA 2.0 / 3.0 / 4.0: credit the photographer and the licence; the edited image is a derivative
  and must be shared under the same licence. Confirm with the site owner that this is acceptable, or
  replace these images with owner photographs.
- CC0 / Public domain: no attribution legally required; credit is still recorded for provenance.
- `Permission unknown` files: the source URL, site and listing title are in `IMAGE_MANIFEST.csv`.
  Obtain permission or credit the retailer before publishing.

## 5. Owner-supplied files - caveat

Owner files were taken from the shop's `Product Images` folder or supplied directly by the owner
during this pass. Provenance is recorded as `Owner-provided`, which is the strongest evidence
available for these files. Authorship was not independently verified. Files that carried a visible
third-party watermark or an obvious marketplace filename were excluded from the approved set even
when they depicted the right product.
